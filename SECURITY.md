# Security

MyDashie runs on your Mac and talks to the services you connect. Keys are stored encrypted (macOS Keychain-backed `safeStorage`) and never leave the machine except to call the service they belong to. Connected-app access is read-only by construction.

Found a vulnerability? Email **security@mydashie.com** with steps to reproduce. Please don't open a public issue for security reports. We aim to respond within three working days.
